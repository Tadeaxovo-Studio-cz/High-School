04 / FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before starting a project.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Websites, landing pages, visual identities, digital products, creative campaigns and experimental web experiences.

How does a project usually start?

Send an email with your idea, goals and timeline. We will reply with a few questions and suggest the best next step.

Do you work with individuals and small teams?

Yes. Tadeax Studio is designed to work closely with creators, startups, small businesses and ambitious independent brands.

How long does a website take?

Small websites can take a few weeks. Larger projects depend on scope, content and feedback speed. A clear estimate comes after the first conversation.

How can I contact support?

Email tad.studio.cz@gmail.com and include as much detail as possible.
